Welcome to Thurso

Further north than the south of Norway and more than 650 miles by road from London, Thurso is the northernmost town on the island of Great Britain.

Nature rules over Caithness. Whether you're here to see Orcas in Scrabster Harbour or the Northern Lights dancing in the dark night skies, Thorfinn provides a warm, comfortable sanctuary for your North Coast adventures.
